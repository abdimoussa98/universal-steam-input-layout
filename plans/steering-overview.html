Steering, in 16 layers.

Right holds W, left holds S. Stick Up selects Modifier 1; Down selects Modifier 0. The opposite driving control selects Modifier 2.

Protected entry

Right Menu + LS click, then release menu. Initial carried WASD has no timeout.

Driving

Press right/left to own W/S. No built-in modifier bank. Release the owner while the opposite is held to switch movement ownership.

Fallback

No driver held: WASD. W warns at 1 second and exits at 2.5 seconds. Release W or acquire a driver to cancel.

Two different scroll behaviors

Normal Modifiers 0/1Modifier 2
Short direction→center scrolls once. Hold or modified action consumes the tap. Same-direction double/hold repeats with Base face buttons.Up/Down immediately scrolls and repeats while held. No tap/double recognition. Face buttons stay Modifier 2.

Leaving Up for sideways removes Modifier 1 silently. Reversing to Down selects Modifier 0 consumed, so it cannot invent a fresh tap. Modifier 0 mirrors this. Before coverage, releasing the Mod 2 opposite control exits all layers to Base. Holding the opposite for 200 ms or using a mapped action applies the existing opposite-side single cover. Covered opposite release restores normal driving without dropping W/S.
